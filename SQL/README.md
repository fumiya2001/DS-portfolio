# Bike Store Sales Analysis

## Objective
This project analyzes store performance, product trends, and shipping delays using SQL.

The goal is to:
- Identify top-performing stores
- Understand product distribution across categories and brands
- Find best-selling categories per store
- Evaluate shipping delays



## Data Overview

The analysis uses the following tables:

- `orders`: order-level information (store, dates)
- `order_items`: product-level sales data (quantity, price, discount)
- `products`: product details
- `categories`: product categories
- `brands`: brand information
- `stores`: store information

This dataset is from kaggle: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/dillonmyrick/bike-store-sample-database/data

##  Analysis 1: Brand Distribution Within Each Category

**Objective**  
Understand how products are distributed across brands within each category.


**Insight**
- Electra is well-known and popular for Children, Comfort, and Cruisers bicycles
- Trek is well-known for Mountain, Road, and Electric bicycles

## Analysis 2: Total Sales by Store

**Objective**  
Compare total revenue generated by each store.


**Insight**
- Baldwin Bikes generated the highest total sales (about $5 M)
- Its sales are approximately 6 times higher than Rowlett store


## Analysis 3: Best-Selling Category per Store

**Objective**  
Identify which product category sells the most in each store.

**Insight**
- Cruisers and Mountain bikes are consistently among the top-selling categories across all stores
- This suggests similar customer preferences regardless of store location


## Analysis 4: Shipping Delay Rate by Store

**Objective**  
Evaluate how often orders are shipped late.

**Insight**
- Rowlett has the smallest delay rate (21.3%)
- The others (Santa Cruz and Baldwin): approximately 29%
